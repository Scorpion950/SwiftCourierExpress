SwiftCourier Express brings the daily work of a courier operation into one place, from booking a parcel to following its delivery and reviewing business activity.

Version: 1.0.0

Shipment Operations

  • Record sender and receiver details, route, parcel dimensions, service type, and charge when booking.
  • Generate a tracking number for each booking and show it on the bill and dashboard.
  • Keep a timestamped history of shipment status changes and remarks.
  • Let customers check shipment progress on a public tracking page.
  • Print a shipment bill or save it as an HTML file.

Business Management

  • View shipment, customer, delivery, and revenue summaries with a recent-shipment list.
  • Search and manage customer records while protecting records linked to shipments.
  • Generate shipment reports by date and status, review totals, and export CSV files.
  • Set company contact details and have them appear on shipment bills.

Designed for Everyday Use

  • Keep management pages behind admin sign-in, with passwords stored as secure hashes.
  • Use a responsive interface with a mobile sidebar menu.
  • Organize the application into separate routes, forms, models, and business services.
